The DMZ inside a company在企業裡當 DMZ

A company puts its public-facing systems — the corporate site, campaign sites, public services — into an isolated zone. Think of it as a front yard: the front yard is designed so it can be hit, and the vault behind it stays untouched. That zone is called a DMZ (demilitarized zone).

09 — What people get wrong

Three things people assume about this work常被誤會的三件事

They think

"A website is a one-off project."

「網站是一次性的專案。」

What I'd say

Building it is maybe a third of the work. Backups, security updates, hosting, content changes and new features are what decide whether the site is still useful in year three. We've been looking after one museum's site since 2020.

建置大概只佔三分之一。備份、安全更新、主機、內容修改、功能擴充，才決定它第三年還能不能用。我們有一個美術館的網站，從 2020 年顧到現在。

They think

"With AI, a website is cheap and fast now."

「現在有 AI，網站又快又便宜。」

What I'd say

Making something look right is faster than ever — that part is true. Getting it live and safe hasn't changed: domain, hosting, SSL, backups, security updates, accessibility, and a CMS your staff can actually use. Being able to build it isn't the same as being able to launch it.

做出一個「看起來對」的東西確實越來越快，這是真的。但「上得了線而且安全」沒有變簡單：網域、主機、SSL、備份、安全更新、無障礙，還有同事真的會用的後台。做得出來，不等於上得了線。

They think

"A website is a design problem — just hire a design agency."

「網站是設計問題，找設計公司就好。」

What I'd say

Design decides the first impression; structure and operations decide whether it still works in year three. I don't compete with design agencies — I work with them. They lead the look, I take it live and keep it running.

設計決定第一印象，架構跟維運決定它第三年還能不能用。我不是跟設計公司搶案子，我是跟他們一起做——他們定方向，我負責落地跟長期維運。

All three collaborations sell the same thing: entrustment, and a transfer of responsibility. Technology depreciates. Accountability doesn't.
三種合作，賣的是同一件事：託付，與責任轉移。技術會貶值，責任不會。
